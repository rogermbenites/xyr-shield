Try it: verify a record

A reveal bundle is what the owner hands an auditor: the record, its salt and its hash. Your browser recomputes sha256(record : salt) and asks NEAR whether that hash was committed, and when.

The demo bundle is a synthetic record committed to xyr-shield-demo.testnet, not a real trade. Click Verify to see it check out, then Tamper to change the price by $1 and watch the proof fail.

Run the rest locally

The trade-log node, attested /ask and the R oracle (with its tamper test, python -m shield.rverify --tamper) run from the repository. See the README.

Data, not advice. XYR Shield publishes data: commitments, aggregate statistics about past records and indicator values with proof of their history. It gives no buy or sell recommendations or investment advice. Past statistics say nothing about future results. · MIT licensed
