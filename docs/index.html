1 The XYR model

Moving averages can’t keep up. Crypto trades 24/7, and traditional indicators don’t adapt to its changing speed.

R adapts. An RSI-based model, three years in the making, that adapts to the speed of bitcoin and crypto markets.

Sessions track it live. The framework follows the cycle in real time, session by session.

ONOvernight, from 3:00 AM ET
OThe open, from 9:30 AM ET
CClose, 4:00 PM ET: daily R locks

Read top-down: Weekly → Daily → Session → Intraday. Each layer closes on a fixed clock, and a closed value must never change.

2 The problem: whose data is right, and who is watching?

Price feeds disagree. Binance and Coinbase print slightly different prices for the same moment, and one price difference changes the model’s values. The model has to be verified on-chain, privately, without exposing how it works.
Traders don’t want to be seen. Visible positions and timing attract predatory market makers. Shielding the trades keeps traders anonymous, and still provable.

Try it: verify a record

A reveal bundle is what the owner hands an auditor: the record, its salt and its hash. Your browser recomputes sha256(record : salt) and asks NEAR whether that hash was committed, and when.

The demo bundle is a synthetic record committed to xyr-shield-demo.testnet, not a real trade. Click Verify to see it check out, then Tamper to change the price by $1 and watch the proof fail.

Run the rest locally

The trade-log node, attested /ask and the R oracle (with its tamper test, python -m shield.rverify --tamper) run from the repository. See the README.
